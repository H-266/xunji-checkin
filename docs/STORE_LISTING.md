## 中文（简体）

**一句话介绍（≤30 字）**

纯离线打卡 + 全局热力日历 + 桌面小组件。

**详细描述**

循记打卡是一款完全离线的打卡与待办工具。它把每天所有任务的完成情况汇成一张全局热力日历：一个格子代表一天，颜色只改变饱和度，完成度越高颜色越浓。你可以按标签筛选、查看年度热力，也可以把今日任务或热力日历放到桌面。

功能：
- 每日打卡、临时待办，支持每周目标、自定义星期、间隔天数、每月日期、每年日期。
- 月/年热力日历、全勤连续、标签筛选、任务补卡、备注与打卡时间。
- 高级统计：周/月/季度/年趋势、标签/类型/时段/周期表现、Top 5。
- 桌面小组件：1×1 迷你进度、2×1/2×2/4×2/4×4 任务卡、2×2/4×2/4×4 热力卡。
- 本地提醒、JSON 全量导入导出、CSV 导出、热力长图、自动备份。
- 五语言界面：简体中文、English、日本語、Français、Español。

隐私：
- 不联网、无账号、无云同步、无广告、无统计 SDK。
- 数据只保存在本机；导出与备份由你通过系统文件选择器指定位置。
- 仅在你开启提醒时申请通知权限。

**关键词**

打卡, 习惯, 待办, 热力图, 日历, 离线, 效率, 桌面小组件, 自律, 记录

## English

**Short description (≤80 chars)**

Offline check-ins, global heatmap calendar, and home-screen widgets.

**Full description**

Xunji Check-in is a fully offline habit and to-do app built around one global heatmap: each cell is a day, and the same hue becomes more saturated as that day's completion rate rises. Filter by tag, browse a full year, and put today's tasks or the heatmap on your home screen.

Features:
- Daily check-ins and one-off todos, plus weekly targets, custom weekdays, every-N-days, monthly dates, and yearly dates.
- Monthly/yearly heatmaps, perfect-day streaks, tag filters, backfill, notes, and check-in timestamps.
- Advanced stats: week/month/quarter/year trends, tag/type/hour/period breakdowns, Top 5.
- Widgets: 1×1 mini progress, 2×1/2×2/4×2/4×4 task cards, 2×2/4×2/4×4 heatmap cards.
- Local reminders, full JSON import/export, CSV export, heatmap long image, automatic backup.
- Five UI languages: Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, French, Spanish.

Privacy: no network, no account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. Data stays on your device. Notification permission is requested only when you enable a reminder.

**Keywords**

check-in, habit, todo, heatmap, calendar, offline, productivity, widget, streak
